Building the future of decentralized mobility on Fetch.ai

HiveRide

In Development

A decentralized cab and ride-booking platform built on the Fetch.ai ecosystem using ASI:One and uAgents, where drivers operate their own autonomous AI agents instead of being controlled by a centralized company.

Each cab, auto-rickshaw, or bike rider creates and owns a uAgent that represents them digitally, handles ride availability, and participates in ride matching. Users book rides directly through ASI:One using natural language, and the system connects them with nearby driver agents in a trust-minimized, agent-to-agent manner.

All payments are conducted using FET tokens, enabling transparent, programmable, and decentralized economic interactions. The platform functions as an open coordination layer for mobility rather than a traditional ride-hailing app, giving drivers ownership, pricing freedom, and autonomy while ensuring fair access and discovery for users.
